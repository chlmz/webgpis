# Surface brief: GPIS site (home + all inner pages)

Scope: full redesign of the 15 public pages + 404. Visitor mode: Persuade on the home page; Read on project, research-line, publications, people, data and impact pages (same world).

Audience: academic researchers and potential partners evaluating collaboration or data access. Job: grasp agenda, evidence and the longitudinal asset fast, then propose a collaboration by e-mail.

Constraints: logo and logo colors binding (navy #0E2347, blue #3489C0, green #5FAA4E); keep every fact and sentence in pt-BR, restructure for scanning and progressive disclosure; static HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages; keep URLs, anchors, sitemap; user asked explicitly for parallax and scroll-driven motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: GPIS as an International Typographic Style identity program: a strict visible 12-column grid, flush-left grotesque at poster scale, logo colors flooding whole regions. Refuses the category default of photo hero + row of equal rounded cards.

OWN-WORLD: White coated stock and edge-to-edge navy fields; logo blue for timeline bands and rules, logo green only for the action. One grotesque family (Archivo) from 0.8rem credits to 6rem headlines, tabular numerals. Hairline navy rules, square corners, no shadows on content, no eyebrows.
