GPIS · IMPACT

Research that gives back
to society.

For us, impact is not just publishing. It is making knowledge circulate, training people, building relationships and bringing research closer to real life.

Research done with families

In workshops with mothers and children followed by the Cohort, we asked what we should observe in interactions (face, gaze, gestures, speech) and what to improve in the wearable camera. These conversations are part of the co-design of the cameras in our study.

We also brought materials for mothers and children to play together, such as the question game “Who are you?”.

Page for Cohort families

We want to do more and more research with people, not just about them.

Knowledge that can be used

Each article becomes a sentence anyone can understand, always with a reference to the published study.

See all publications

  1. Brazilian Journal of Psychiatry · 2021

    Mothers' mental health before and during the pandemic

    At the first assessment during the pandemic, 29.3% of mothers had probable depression, 25.9% probable generalised anxiety and 40.5% moderate or severe stress. Depression and anxiety increased compared with the period before the pandemic.

    Read the article ↗

  2. Brazilian Journal of Psychiatry · 2023

    Fears about their children and maternal mental health

    Concern about their children's future and fear of passing COVID-19 on to them were associated with higher trajectories of maternal depression and anxiety.

    Read the article ↗

  3. Revista de Neuro-Psiquiatría · 2025

    Division of household tasks and maternal anxiety

    A more even division of household tasks was associated with a lower prevalence of moderate or severe anxiety among mothers.

    Read the article ↗

Research built as a network

Explore the network

Collaborative networks broaden questions, methods and perspectives, and make research more robust. We work with partners in the United Kingdom, Chile and Peru.

What we want to build next

We want every finding to return to those who made the research possible.

We are building a longitudinal research platform more connected with society: communities taking part in decisions, findings returned in an understandable way and science closer to everyday life, without giving up scientific rigour.
